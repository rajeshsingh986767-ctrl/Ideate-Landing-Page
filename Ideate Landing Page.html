About — Since 1998

Science and art
were never two disciplines

Techfest began in 1998 as a handful of IIT Bombay students who believed invention deserved the same stage as spectacle. Three decades on, it has grown into Asia's largest science and technology festival — a three-day convergence of over 175,000 minds, drawn from more than 2,500 colleges across India and 500 overseas.

Beyond the competitions and the crowds, Techfest carries a UNESCO- and UNICEF-backed mandate: to turn curiosity into consequence, all year round — not just for three days in December.
